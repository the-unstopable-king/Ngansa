Poulet DG

Ingredients:
- Chicken pieces
- Plantains (ripe, fried)
- Tomatoes, onions, bell peppers
- Garlic, ginger
- Oil, salt, pepper

Instructions:
1. Fry plantains until golden.
2. Season and fry chicken until browned.
3. Make tomato sauce with onions, peppers, garlic, ginger.
4. Add chicken and plantains into sauce.
5. Simmer together until flavors blend.
