Egusi Soup

Ingredients:
- Egusi seeds (melon seeds)
- Palm oil
- Meat (goat or beef)
- Stockfish
- Spinach or bitterleaf
- Seasoning cubes, salt, pepper

Instructions:
1. Roast and grind egusi seeds.
2. Heat palm oil, add ground egusi, fry until fragrant.
3. Add meat, stockfish, water, and seasonings.
4. Simmer until meat is tender.
5. Add spinach or bitterleaf, cook briefly.
