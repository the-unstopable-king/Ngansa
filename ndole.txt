Ndolé

Ingredients:
- Bitterleaf (washed)
- Ground peanuts
- Beef or shrimp
- Palm oil
- Seasoning cubes, salt, pepper

Instructions:
1. Boil bitterleaf until softened.
2. Cook ground peanuts into a paste.
3. Combine bitterleaf, peanut paste, and palm oil.
4. Add beef or shrimp, simmer until cooked.
5. Season to taste.
