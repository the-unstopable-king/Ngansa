Achu Soup

Ingredients:
- Cocoyams
- Palm oil
- Limestone (kanwa)
- Meat or fish
- Seasoning cubes, salt, pepper

Instructions:
1. Boil cocoyams, pound into smooth paste.
2. Mix palm oil with limestone water to form yellow soup.
3. Add meat or fish stock.
4. Serve soup with pounded cocoyam (achu).
5. Season to taste.
